# Quick-scan checklist (50 checks)

Use when the user wants "just the main problems" instead of a scored audit. Run every check, then list only the ❌ items, grouped by section. No scores, no rewrite table, no next steps.

## 1. Hero (6)
- [ ] Title names the category and promise (not just a model name)?
- [ ] Subheadline adds audience, context or benefit (doesn't restate the title)?
- [ ] First image is the most compelling angle?
- [ ] Price visible above the fold?
- [ ] Rating anchor ("4.6★ from N reviews") above the fold?
- [ ] Commits to one emotion (mobile)?

## 2. Images (6)
- [ ] At least 5 images (ideally 8+)?
- [ ] Close-up of material or detail?
- [ ] In-scale image (in hand, on body, next to a familiar object)?
- [ ] In-context / in-use image?
- [ ] Customer photos?
- [ ] Mobile carousel swipeable, with a position indicator and pinch-to-zoom?

## 3. Video (4)
- [ ] A product video exists?
- [ ] It's a demo or explainer, not just a brand ad?
- [ ] Strong first 3 seconds, captions on?
- [ ] Placed high (above the fold or 2nd in the gallery)?

## 4. Benefit copy (5)
- [ ] Each feature translated into what the buyer gets?
- [ ] No empty claims ("premium", "innovative", "high quality")?
- [ ] Written to "you"?
- [ ] Vitamin vs painkiller strategy matches the product?
- [ ] Spec table (if any) grouped and scannable?

## 5. Social proof (6)
- [ ] At least 3 kinds of proof (rating, detailed reviews, customer photos, press, third-party platform)?
- [ ] Average rating believable (not a perfect 5.0 from a handful)?
- [ ] Some 4-star (or lower) reviews visible?
- [ ] "Verified buyer" labels, names, dates?
- [ ] Reviews from the last ~3 months?
- [ ] Reviews sortable or filterable?

## 6. FAQ (4)
- [ ] FAQ on the product page (not only in the footer)?
- [ ] Covers shipping, returns, fit/compatibility, care?
- [ ] Covers the category-specific question buyers always ask?
- [ ] "Still have questions?" contact option?

## 7. Trust & risk reversal (6)
- [ ] Returns policy visible on the page?
- [ ] Shipping cost and delivery estimate shown before checkout?
- [ ] Payment icons and security reassurance?
- [ ] Contact info with hours or response time?
- [ ] Guarantee language near the CTA?
- [ ] No fake scarcity (fake countdown, fake "only N left")?

## 8. CTA (5)
- [ ] Verb-led and specific (not bare "Buy now" / "Submit")?
- [ ] Sentence case, not Title Case or CAPS?
- [ ] Sticky add-to-cart on mobile?
- [ ] High contrast against its background?
- [ ] Risk-reducing line beneath (returns, shipping)?

## 9. Design (4)
- [ ] Text contrast ≥ 4.5:1?
- [ ] ≤ 2 typefaces?
- [ ] Clear hierarchy between headline, subhead and body?
- [ ] No horizontal tabs hiding description, specs or reviews?

## 10. Mobile (2)
- [ ] Body ≥ 16px, tap targets ≥ 44px with spacing?
- [ ] Loads in under ~3 seconds; pop-ups non-intrusive?

## 11–12. Hooks & cross-sell (2)
- [ ] Any hook used is genuine and fits the category?
- [ ] Relevant "frequently bought together" or bundle with the saving stated?

## Output

```
Quick scan: X issues

🚫 Hero (2)
• No rating in the hero → add "★ [real rating] from [real count] reviews" under the title
• Subheadline repeats the title → use it to name who it's for

🚫 Trust (1)
• Returns only in the footer → add "Free 60-day returns" under the add-to-cart button
```
